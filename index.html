06 — Before You Come

WHAT TO BRING
TO THE COUNTER

Five things make the visit short and the part right. The counter can work with any one of them — with all five, there is almost nothing to get wrong.

B—01

Model & year

“Mehran 2012” beats “Mehran”. The same model can take different parts across years.

B—02

The old part

The surest match there is. Put it in a bag, bring it in, hold it next to the new one.

B—03

A photo of the part

If the old part is still on the car, photograph it — and the label or part number on it.

B—04

Chassis number

Your mechanic can read it off the car. It settles arguments about which version you have.

B—05

Your mechanic's word

Ask your mechanic to write down the part name. Bring the note — it saves a second trip.

B—06

Or just call first

One call to (051) 5564724 with the model and year tells you whether the trip is worth it.

09 — Straight Talk

CHECK BEFORE
YOU BUY

Spare parts come in different makes and grades, and prices move with the market — so this shop doesn't print fixed prices or make promises about a part's origin on a poster. Three habits protect every buyer on Kashmir Road:

  1. Ask what make it is — and what it isn't — before you pay.
  2. Match it against your old part at the counter: size, bolt holes, connectors.
  3. Keep the bill with the part until your mechanic has fitted it and the car runs.

If a part doesn't match, say so at the counter before it is fitted — not after.
